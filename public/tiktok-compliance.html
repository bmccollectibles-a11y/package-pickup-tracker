TikTok Sales Tool Privacy and Compliance

Last updated May 11, 2026

Operator and Scope

BMC Collectibles operates TikTok Sales Tool, an internal reporting tool used to process TikTok Shop order and reporting data for sales attribution, shift reporting, product and order summaries, refunds, reconciliation, and operational reporting.

Data We May Process

TikTok Sales Tool may process order identifiers, package identifiers, product and SKU details, order status, quantities, refund details, buyer username or nickname where provided by TikTok, recipient and shipping-related fields where provided, payment method labels, transaction timestamps, employee shift schedules, and import metadata.

How We Use Data

BMC Collectibles uses this data for sales reporting, shift attribution, order reconciliation, refund and product summaries, accounting and tax records, customer support, fraud and security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

Privacy Commitments

BMC Collectibles does not sell TikTok-derived personal data and does not use TikTok-derived data for third-party advertising, unrelated profiling, credit decisions, or automated decisions about end users. Data is used only for internal TikTok Shop sales reporting, reconciliation, and operations.

Security and Location

Data processed by TikTok Sales Tool is stored and processed in the United States. BMC Collectibles protects TikTok-derived data using access control, least privilege, secure transmission via HTTPS/TLS, authorized devices and systems, credential protection, endpoint and device security, authentication, screen lock, software updates, and incident response procedures. Credentials are protected and are not stored in public repositories.

Data Requests and Breach Notification

BMC Collectibles assists TikTok, TikTok Shop, sellers, users, and authorized representatives with lawful data access, correction, deletion, update, restriction, or export requests. BMC Collectibles maintains incident response and breach notification procedures to alert TikTok, sellers, or users when required for suspected or identified data breaches.

Retention and Deletion

Data is retained only as needed for business, accounting, legal, dispute, fraud prevention, security, tax, and compliance purposes. When data is no longer needed, BMC Collectibles deletes or anonymizes it. At the end of the TikTok API relationship, BMC Collectibles will delete TikTok-derived customer and user data unless retention is required for legal, accounting, tax, dispute, fraud prevention, security, or compliance purposes.
