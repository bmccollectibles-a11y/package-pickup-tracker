Privacy Policy

Last updated May 10, 2026

Package Pickup Alerts

BMC Package Tracker sends package pickup and delivery status alerts to internal BMC staff members. Messages are used for operational package receiving only and are not used for marketing.

Information We Collect

The tracker stores tracking numbers, package notes, package status, and staff phone numbers entered by authorized users for text alerts.

How We Use Information

We use this information to monitor package status, show the package dashboard, send pickup alerts, and keep received packages out of future refresh checks.

Sharing

Phone numbers and package alert information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. We use service providers such as Shippo, Resend, Twilio, and Render only to operate the package tracker and send alerts.

Opt Out

Recipients can opt out of SMS alerts by asking an authorized dashboard user to remove their phone number from the Text recipients list. Recipients may also reply STOP to opt out when supported by the messaging provider.

Contact

For privacy questions or text-alert changes, contact BMC through the internal package tracker administrator.
