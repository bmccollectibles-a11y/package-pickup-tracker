Program Description

BMC Package Tracker provides low-volume operational alerts about package pickup and delivery status. Alerts are sent to internal BMC staff members whose phone numbers are added by authorized users.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on package activity. Messages may be sent when packages are ready for pickup, out for delivery, or included in a manual pickup alert.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

Opt Out

To stop receiving SMS alerts, ask an authorized dashboard user to remove your phone number from the Text recipients list. You may also reply STOP to opt out when supported by the messaging provider.

Help

For help, contact the internal package tracker administrator or reply HELP when supported by the messaging provider.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details about how alert information is used.
