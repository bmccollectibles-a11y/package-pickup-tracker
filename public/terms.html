Program Description

BMC Package Tracker provides low-volume operational alerts about package pickup and delivery status. Alerts are sent to authorized internal BMC staff members who have opted in to receive package notification messages.

SMS Consent

SMS consent is optional. End users opt in through BMC's internal package tracking/admin tool by entering their mobile phone number, checking an unchecked SMS consent checkbox, and saving their profile. SMS consent is not shared, sold, or transferred to third parties.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on package activity. Messages may be sent when packages are ready for pickup, out for delivery, or included in a manual package alert.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

Opt Out

To stop receiving SMS alerts, reply STOP. You may also ask an authorized dashboard user to remove your phone number from the Text recipients list.

Help

For help, reply HELP or contact the internal package tracker administrator.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details about how alert information is used.
